Join free How it works

Joining is free, and free members aren't ID-checked. Club+ members verify their identity through a specialist verification provider, and can add a short video introduction and link a social account. Each check shows as a small badge on their profile, so you can always see who has been verified and who hasn't.

With Club+ you can also choose to connect only with other verified members.

You choose who to message and when, and nothing is shared until you decide to. No one can promise that every meeting goes perfectly, so we'd always suggest meeting somewhere public first and letting a friend know your plans.

We look at where you're going and when, how you like to travel, and what you're into. Members heading to the same place around the same dates rise to the top, and every profile shows exactly what you have in common.

No one is introduced automatically. You browse your shortlist and decide who you'd like to say hello to. See how it works for the full picture.
