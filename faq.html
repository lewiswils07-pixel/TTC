Questions and answers

The things people most often ask before joining.

Who is Together Travel Club for?

Anyone over 18 who would like company on a trip, or simply someone to meet when they get there. Many of our members travel on their own.

Is it safe?

You choose who to talk to, and nobody is introduced to you automatically. Every profile shows whether the member's ID has been checked, and Club+ members can choose to meet verified members only. We always suggest meeting somewhere public first and letting a friend know your plans.

How much does it cost?

Joining is free. Club+ costs £7.99 a month or £59.99 a year, and adds ID checks, no monthly limit on new people and the option to meet verified members only. Founding members get 3 months of Club+ free. See prices.

Do I have to travel with the people I meet?

No. You can just chat, meet for a coffee, spend a day together or travel together. It's entirely up to you, and you can stop a conversation at any time.

Can I cancel or leave?

Yes, at any time. If you cancel Club+, you keep it until the end of the period you've paid for. If you close your account, your profile is removed straight away.

What do you do with my details?

We only use them to run the club: to show you people going your way, to let you chat, and to check your ID if you choose Club+. We never sell your details. Read our privacy policy.
