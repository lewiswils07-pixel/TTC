# Intent: Together Travel Club members platform

Confirmed with Lewis on 3 October 2026, through an interview held before the spec was written.
The spec (`docs/spec/SPEC-members-platform.md`) and the build plan (`tasks/plan.md`)
both follow from this document. If this changes, update it first.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Outcome** | A working members' platform, website first, with iPhone and Android apps close behind, built from the same code. Members build a profile and add trips. They are shown people going to the same place at overlapping dates, with the best interest matches first. With no trip planned, they find people with similar interests to plan the next one. Connection is mutual opt-in. Once connected, members chat, form small groups around shared trips, and plan with an AI trip planner. |
| **Users** | Solo travellers, mainly an older audience. Safety and simplicity come before feature count. |
| **Free vs Club+** | Hinge-style split. Free has basic filters (destination, dates, age range, gender preference, distance from home) and monthly limits on connections and trip plans. Club+ adds an ID-verified badge, advanced filters (verified-only, travel style, budget, specific interests, pace), more connections and unlimited trip plans. |
| **Safety (day one)** | Mutual opt-in before chat. Phone check for everyone. Block and report on every profile, message and group, feeding a review list that Lewis works through. Location at city level only. Warnings when someone asks for money or tries to move off the app. Meeting-up guidance and an option to share your plan with a trusted contact. "Did you meet?" feedback after a trip. |
| **Why now** | Launch around mid-November 2026, within about 6 weeks of 3 October. |
| **Success** | Real members can sign up, match, connect, chat, form a group and plan a trip from start to finish, on phone and computer, with no errors. It passes the same launch checks the marketing site passed, and the apps are submitted to both stores. |
| **Constraints** | 6 weeks. Running costs of about £100 a month before revenue, with more acceptable within reason. One set of code for the website and the apps. No secret keys in the website or app. |
| **Out of scope for launch** | Video calls. Photo swap, calendar export and "Meet for a day". The marketing plan, which Lewis runs separately. Advertising the in-app extras on the public pages, which stay low-key inside the app. |

## Changes after the first summary

- Groups moved **into** the launch.
- The budget can go above £100 a month "within reason".
- The website says "App coming soon" if the apps are not approved in time. Launching together is preferred.
