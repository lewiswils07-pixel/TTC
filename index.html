More about how it works

Safety comes first

You stay in control from the first hello to the first meeting.

  • See who's ID-checked. Every profile shows whether the member has verified their identity.
  • You choose who to talk to. Nobody is introduced to you automatically.
  • Meet in public first. We always suggest a café or another busy place for a first meeting.
  • Tell us if anything feels wrong. We'll look into it straight away.
