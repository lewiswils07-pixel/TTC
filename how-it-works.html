1 Step one

Tell us about you

Start with your name, email and where you're based, then add the parts that help us find your people. It takes a couple of minutes, and you can add more whenever you like.

  • Your interests. Street food, hiking, galleries, live music: the most honest early signal of who you'd get on with.
  • Your travel style. Budget, pace, and whether you like every day planned or prefer to wing it.
  • Your trips. Where you're going and roughly when, or just where you'd like to go next.

2 Step two

Get your connections

We look for members whose plans and preferences overlap with yours, and put them on a shortlist you can browse at your own pace.

  • Same place, similar time. People heading to the same destination on overlapping dates rise to the top.
  • Same way of travelling. Solo, as a couple or with friends; slow days or packed itineraries.
  • What you share, highlighted. Every profile shows exactly what you have in common, so you can see why you've been connected.

No one is introduced automatically. You decide who you'd like to say hello to.

3 Step three

Chat, then meet

Once you've found someone you'd like to travel with, chat for as long as you need before deciding anything.

  • Message your connections. Talk through plans, dates and expectations before anyone commits.
  • Meet before you go. If you're both nearby, grab a coffee before the trip. It's entirely optional.
  • Travel your way. Share a whole itinerary, meet up for a day or two, or just have someone to call when you land.
