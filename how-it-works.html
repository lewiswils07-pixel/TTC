How it works

Three simple steps, and you're in control at every one.

  1. 1

    Tell us about yourself

    Sign up with your name and email, then add where you're travelling and roughly when. Add a few things you enjoy, like walking, food, museums or music, so we can find people you'll get on with.

  2. 2

    See who's going your way

    We show you a short list of members heading to the same place at a similar time. Each profile shows what you have in common, so it's easy to decide who to say hello to. Nobody is introduced to you automatically.

  3. 3

    Say hello, then meet if you'd like

    Chat in the club first and take as long as you need. If you get on, meet for a coffee before you go, explore together for a day, or travel together. It's entirely up to you.

Staying safe

Good habits, whichever plan you're on.

  • Look for the Verified badge. It means the member has had their ID checked.
  • Take your time. Chat in the club for as long as you like before sharing personal details.
  • Meet somewhere public. A busy café is ideal for a first hello.
  • Tell someone, and tell us. Let a friend know your plans, and report anyone who makes you uncomfortable.
