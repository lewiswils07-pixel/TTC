How it works

From a trip idea to a travel companion.

Together Travel Club is built around four simple steps. You tell us a little about yourself and where you're headed, we find members who travel the way you do, everyone is verified before they can connect, and you take it from there, at your own pace.

  1. 1

    Step 1

    Tell us about you

    Start with the basics (your name, email and where you're based), then build out the parts that help us find your people. It takes a couple of minutes, and you can add more whenever you like.

    • Your interests. Street food, hiking, galleries, live music: what you're into is the most honest early signal of who you'd get on with.
    • Your travel style. Budget, pace, board basis, and whether you like every day planned or prefer to wing it.
    • Your trips. Where you're going and roughly when, or just the places you'd like to go next.

    Nothing beyond the basics is required to join. Each extra detail simply sharpens your shortlist.

  2. 2

    Step 2

    We find your people

    We look for members whose plans and preferences overlap with yours, then put together a shortlist you can browse at your own pace.

    • Same place, similar time. Members heading to the same destination around the same dates rise to the top.
    • Same way of travelling. Solo, as a couple or with friends; slow days or packed itineraries; hostels or hotels.
    • Shared interests, highlighted. Every profile shows exactly what you have in common, so you can see why you've been matched.

    No one is introduced automatically. You decide who you'd like to say hello to.

  3. 3

    Step 3

    Everyone is verified

    Every member is checked before they can connect with anyone, so you always know the person you're talking to is who they say they are.

    • ID verified. Identity checks run through a specialist verification provider, so your documents never sit on our site.
    • Video intro'd. Members can add a short video introduction, so you can put a face and a voice to a profile.
    • Social linked. Members can link a social account for an extra layer of reassurance.

    You'll see these as small badges on each profile, so it's always clear what's been checked.

  4. 4

    Step 4

    Message, then meet

    Once you've found someone you'd like to travel with, chat for as long as you need before deciding anything.

    • Message directly. Talk through plans, dates and expectations before anyone commits to anything.
    • Meet before you go. If you're both nearby, grab a coffee before the trip. It's entirely optional.
    • Travel your way. Share a whole itinerary, meet up for a day or two, or just have someone to call when you land.

    Our tip for a first meeting: choose somewhere public, and let a friend know your plans.
