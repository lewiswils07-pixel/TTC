Terms of service

1. Acceptance of these terms

By creating an account or using Together Travel Club ("the club", "we", "us"), you agree to these terms. If you don't agree, please don't use the club.

2. Who can join

You must be at least 18 years old and able to enter into a binding agreement. You may hold only one account, and the details you give us must be accurate and your own.

3. Free membership, Club+ and verification

Joining is free. Club+ is an optional paid subscription that adds identity verification and other features described on the membership page. Club+ renews until you cancel; if you cancel, it stays active until the end of the period you've paid for.

Free members are not identity-verified. Club+ members can complete identity verification, which is carried out by a third-party verification provider on our behalf, and each profile shows whether its member is verified.

Verification confirms that a member is who they say they are at the time of checking. It is not a background check, and it doesn't guarantee how anyone will behave.

4. Member conduct

Treat other members with respect. You agree not to harass, threaten, deceive or discriminate against anyone; not to use the club for commercial solicitation, dating services or anything unlawful; and not to share another member's details without their consent.

You're responsible for your own decisions about whether, when and how to meet or travel with other members. Please take sensible precautions, such as meeting somewhere public first.

5. Account suspension and termination

We may suspend or close an account that breaks these terms, puts other members at risk, or fails verification. Where it's safe and appropriate to do so, we'll tell you why.

You can close your account at any time. Closing it removes your profile from other members' shortlists straight away; see the privacy policy below for what we keep and for how long.

6. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms as the club develops. If we make significant changes, we'll let members know before they take effect.

7. Prototype disclaimer

Together Travel Club is currently a prototype. Features described on this site may be incomplete, may change, or may not yet exist. The club is provided "as is", without guarantees of availability or fitness for any particular purpose.

Privacy policy

1. What we collect

Account details: your name, email address and home location.

Profile details you choose to add: interests, travel style, trips and destinations, and any video introduction or linked social account.

Messages you send to other members, and basic technical data such as device, browser and log information needed to run the site securely.

2. How we use it

We use your data only to run the club: to verify you, to build your shortlist of compatible members, to let you message other members, to keep the community safe, and to tell you about important changes. We don't sell your personal data.

3. Identity verification

Identity documents are processed by our verification provider, not stored on this site. We receive the result of the check, and keep only what we need to show that verification took place.

4. Cookies

We use essential cookies to keep you signed in and the site secure. We'll only use analytics or other non-essential cookies with your consent, and you can change your choice at any time.

5. Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is open. When you close your account, we delete or anonymise your personal data within [retention period to be confirmed], except where we need to keep something longer for legal, safety or fraud-prevention reasons.

6. Your rights and privacy requests

You can ask to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, or object to how we use it. To make a request, contact us at [privacy contact email to be confirmed]. We'll respond within [response time to be confirmed].

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change how we use your data, we'll update this policy and tell members before the changes take effect.
