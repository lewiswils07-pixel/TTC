How this grows up

Shared interests get you a shortlist. The rest sharpens it.

What you're seeing here is the first layer only — shared interests among a founding list of fourteen fictional profiles. A real match also weighs travel style, budget and pace, actual dates and destinations, and runs against a verified member base rather than a handful of stand-ins. Interests just happen to be the most honest starting signal: what you're into travels with you, trip to trip.
