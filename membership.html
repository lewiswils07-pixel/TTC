Why there's a free version

A travel club is only as good as the people in it. Keeping the basics free means more people heading your way, whichever version you choose.

Verified means checked

Free members aren't ID-checked. Every profile shows clearly whether someone is verified, and with Club+ you can choose to connect only with people who are.

Cancel any time

Cancel Club+ whenever you like and keep it until the end of the period you've paid for. You can also close your account at any time, for any reason.
