Membership

Join the founding community.

We're building Together Travel Club with its first members, not just for them. Founding members get the full club while we grow it, and a say in what it becomes.

Founding membership

Free

Free for the whole founding phase, while we build the community. There are no paid tiers yet.

What's included

  • Verified matching

    A shortlist of members heading where you're heading, or who travel the way you do, with what you have in common shown on every profile.

  • Unlimited browsing

    Look through as many profiles and destinations as you like. There's no cap on how many trips you can follow.

  • Direct messaging

    Message any member on your shortlist, and take as long as you need before deciding to meet or travel together.

  • Everyone verified

    Every member you can contact has been through identity checks, with video intros and linked socials shown as badges.

Become a founding member

Why it's free right now

A travel club is only as good as the people in it. While the founding community grows, we'd rather everyone could join, match and message freely than put a paywall in front of the first members.

If that changes

Any future pricing hasn't been decided yet. If we introduce paid features, founding members will hear about it well before it happens, and you'll never be charged for anything you haven't chosen.

Leaving is easy

You can close your account at any time, for any reason. There's nothing to cancel and no notice period.
