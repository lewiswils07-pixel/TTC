Travel felt lonelier than it should.

Together Travel Club helps people who are heading to the same places, and enjoy the same things, find each other.

It started in Paris

On a trip to Paris I kept noticing the same thing. At every landmark there were people travelling on their own, holding their phones at arm's length, trying to take a photo of themselves with nobody to ask.

Hundreds of people, in the same place, all on their own.

Plenty of them would probably have got on. They'd chosen the same city, the same sights and often the same week, but there was no easy, safe way for them to find each other.

So I started Together Travel Club: a simple way to meet people heading where you're heading, who enjoy what you enjoy, with ID checks there when you'd like the reassurance.

Lewis Wilson, founder

What we believe

  • Interests before photos

    Profiles lead with what you enjoy and how you like to travel, not with looks.

  • Safety you can see

    Every profile shows whether someone's ID has been checked.

  • You set the pace

    Chat for as long as you like. Meet if you want to, or don't.

  • Built with our members

    Our founding members help decide what we build next.
