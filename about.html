About us

Travel felt lonelier than it should.

We built Together Travel Club because turning up somewhere new on your own still feels harder than it should. Group tours can feel over-organised, dating apps aren't built for finding a hiking partner or a fellow gallery-hopper, and most of us know someone who's quietly cancelled a trip because they didn't want to go alone. We wanted a simple way to find people who are headed to the same place, or who travel the way you do, before you've even left home.

The club works more like a members' community than a dating app: joining is free, members can verify their identity with Club+, profiles are built around interests and travel style rather than photos, and conversations start because you're both going somewhere, not because an algorithm paired you up. Whether you've already booked or you're just curious who else feels the pull towards a place, Together Travel Club is where the "who" gets sorted before the "where."
