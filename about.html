It started in Paris.

On a trip to Paris I kept noticing the same thing. At every landmark there were solo travellers holding their phones at arm's length, trying to take a photo of themselves with nobody to ask.

Hundreds of people, in the same place, all on their own.

Plenty of them would probably have got on. They'd chosen the same city, the same sights and often the same week, but there was no easy, safe way for them to find each other.

So I started Together Travel Club: a place to connect with people heading where you're heading, matched on what you're actually into, with verification there when you'd like the reassurance.

Lewis Wilson, Founder

A few things we won't compromise on.

  1. 01

    Interests before photos

    Profiles lead with what you love doing and how you like to travel. No swiping on looks.

  2. 02

    Safety you can see

    Every profile shows whether someone is ID verified, and Club+ lets you connect with verified members only.

  3. 03

    You set the pace

    Nobody is introduced automatically. Chat for as long as you like, meet if you want to, or don't.

  4. 04

    Built with our members

    We're growing the club with its first members, and founding members get a real say in what it becomes.
